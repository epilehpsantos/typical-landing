It sounds like you

Typical learns your voice from your own writing — your rhythm, your vocabulary, your emoji habits — and matches it. A sales intensity dial from 1 to 5 decides how hard the message sells: at 1 it answers the question in a sentence, at 5 it builds the case and closes.

It knows who you're talking to

Add your clients and your listings once. Typical uses what it knows — budget, interests, what you last discussed, which properties they asked about — to write something specific instead of something generic. Ask for a recap before you reply and catch up on a client in one card.

It never guesses

If an action needs to know which listing or which client, Typical asks instead of inventing. Price and terms for what? It wants the answer before it writes.

What it does

Everything from the keyboard. Nothing to open.

  • Reply to any client message in your voice
  • Handle objections, pitch value, quote price and terms
  • Translate a message both ways without leaving the chat
  • Recap a client's history before you answer
  • Draft follow-ups for clients who went quiet
  • Schedule visits, calls and dinners from a typed sentence
  • Send email from the app with your Gmail account

Built for high-ticket selling

Real estate, luxury goods, B2B services — any sale where the message you send matters more than the volume you send.

Typical is made for people who write few messages, each worth a lot. If your work is funnels and chatbots, this isn't for you — and that's the point.

Privacy

Typical never reads your conversations.

Typical is a keyboard extension. Apple's sandbox blocks any keyboard from reading other apps. It works from what you copy and what you type into it — nothing else. You review everything before it sends. AI drafts; you decide.
